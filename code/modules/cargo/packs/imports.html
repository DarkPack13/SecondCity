/obj/item/paper/fluff/certificateImports category. This is for crates not intended for goodies, but also not intended for departmental orders. This allows us to have a few crates meant for deliberate purchase through cargo, and for cargo to have a few items they explicitly control. It also holds all of the black market material and contraband material, including items meant for purchase only through emagging the console.
