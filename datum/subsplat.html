Subsplat
/datum/subsplat

Represents the "splats" described in splatbooks, e.g. clans, tribes, factions and kiths. These are the "real" splats but we call them subsplats as we used that type path for what WW calls supernaturals A more official term sometimes used, especially for games in the Chronicles of Darkness, is "character axis".

These at present are only stored on splats as a var for a given subtype and requires mostly implementing its own handling for everything.

Proc Details

proc
apply_after_setup
(/mob/living/carbon/human/source)

Applies anything that should only be applied when joining the round through late-start or round-start This exludes spawning as a ghost and other such sources. This is for stuff like outfits or teleporting players to locations.

Arguments:

  • source - Human with this subsplat joining the round.

proc
on_join_round
(/mob/living/carbon/human/joining)

Applies subsplat-specific effects when the mob that has the subsplat logs into their mob at roundstart. Anything that's not innate to the subsplat and more part of its social structure or whatnot should go in here. Will give them their subsplat keys by default.

Arguments:

  • joining - Human with this subsplat joining the round.

proc
on_lose
(/mob/living/carbon/human/losing_mob)

Undoes the effects of on_gain more or less remove the effects of gaining the subsplat.

Arguments:

  • losing_mob - Human losing the subsplat.
